## P1 — exclusion bypassed the fixed acknowledgement join

Round 11 made *acknowledgement* locator-aware, but exclusion keys on a
different question: "does this source carry any ERR?". So the same
misdirected issue still worked, from the other side:

```
a healthy upstream record carries MALFORMED_ENVELOPE/ERR
whose locator names .warrants/blobs/p
  -> validator []   projector []   record vanishes from the graph
  -> exclusions[] pairs the record's path with an issue about another file
```

A sound record erased, and the exclusion entry — the artifact that exists to
say which member was dropped and why — carrying a foreign coordinate as its
evidence.

The ordering lesson is worth keeping: fixing the join was necessary and not
sufficient, because **acknowledgement and exclusion are two different
consumers of the same list**, and only one of them had been taught to read
the locator.

**Disposition — the reviewer's v0 rule, adopted verbatim.** A source's
issues are local to that source: a `path` locator MUST equal the source's
own path; `json-pointer` and `byte-range` are relative to it; `global` is
not permitted in `sources[].issues[]` at all, since store-wide subjects
belong in `global_issues[]` where they are attached to no member. Findings:
`ISSUE_OUT_OF_SCOPE`, `GLOBAL_ISSUE_ON_SOURCE`. Recorded as rev 15 of the
proposal.

Four vectors: the exact countervector (sound record + misdirected ERR), a
`global` locator on a source, and two positive controls — a correctly
scoped `path` issue and a `json-pointer` issue into the record itself.
